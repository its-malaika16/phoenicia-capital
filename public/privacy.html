Privacy Policy

Phoenicia Capital Holdings uses the details you send through the contact form to read and reply to your enquiry. That form asks for your name, email address, organization, enquiry type, and message.

Those details are sent to support@phoeniciacapital.co.uk. They are not sold. They are kept only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any follow-up you ask for.

To ask for a copy of your message, or to ask us to delete it, email support@phoeniciacapital.co.uk or call +971 50 429 4194.
