# Phoenicia Capital Holdings

Phoenicia Capital Holdings is a privately held company led by founder and CEO Ali A Hammoud. It owns and operates four ventures:

- TeachWise, a recruitment platform for schools and specialist sectors
- Petrovate, a U.S. and Middle East energy and AI well-optimization company
- Cedar Capital Bank, a digital banking platform for banks and fintechs
- Cedar Payroll, an HMRC-compliant payroll service in the United Kingdom
