Phoenicia Capital Holdings

Phoenicia Capital Holdings is a privately held company that owns and operates TeachWise, Petrovate, Cedar Capital Bank, and Cedar Payroll. The group is led by founder and CEO Ali A Hammoud and works across education, energy, banking, and payroll.

Ventures

  • TeachWise is a recruitment platform for schools and specialist sectors.
  • Petrovate is a U.S. and Middle East energy and AI well-optimization company.
  • Cedar Capital Bank is a digital banking platform for banks and fintechs.
  • Cedar Payroll is an HMRC-compliant payroll service in the United Kingdom.
